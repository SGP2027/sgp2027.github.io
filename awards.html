Awards & support

The preliminary call for papers provides for three best paper awards and a software award recognizing an influential open-source project. Papers accompanied by a complete open-source implementation may also receive a reproducibility stamp.

Award nominations may be sent to awardnominations@geometryprocessing.org.

Sponsors and any student support opportunities will be announced once confirmed.
