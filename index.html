Symposium on Geometry Processing 2027

June 21–25, 2027 · Singapore · International Geometry Summit 2027

The Symposium on Geometry Processing brings together researchers developing the mathematical foundations and computational tools for understanding and working with geometric data.

Spanning mathematics, computer science and engineering, the community explores the acquisition, modeling, analysis, manipulation and simulation of digital shapes.

The Eurographics Symposium on Geometry Processing 2027 will take place on June 21–25, 2027 in Singapore, as part of the International Geometry Summit 2027. The SGP Graduate School will precede the conference on June 19–20, 2027.
